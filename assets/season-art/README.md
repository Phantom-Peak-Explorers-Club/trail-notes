# Season artwork

These 17 covers were generated for the Canada Water trail archive with the built-in image generation tool. Each image features Jonabot and Jonabot Jr in an illustrated scene based on that season's trails. The site adds the approved season name, year and “Canada Water” as an HTML/CSS overlay, so the text stays crisp and can be changed without editing the art.

## Shared image brief

> Create one premium cinematic landscape illustration for a Phantom Peak trail archive. Preserve Jonabot and Jonabot Jr from the supplied character references. Ground the scene in the real Canada Water venue: a narrow canal, two-level wooden walkways, colourful frontier-style shop fronts, waterfall, festoon lights and theatrical overhead lighting. Show both robots exploring season-specific story clues. Use a detailed storybook style and leave room at the top and bottom for a later text overlay. Do not include lettering, logos, watermarks or borders.

Reference images: the user's Jonabot and Jonabot Jr graphics in `Phantom Peak Stuff`; the user's photo of the Canada Water indoor town. The venue treatment was also checked against [Phantom Peak's official experience photos](https://www.phantompeak.com/the-experience/what-is-phantom-peak/).
