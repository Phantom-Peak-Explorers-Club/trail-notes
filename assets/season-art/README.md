# Canada Water season artwork

These 17 covers were generated for the trail archive. Each features Jonabot and Jonabot Jr, based on the character references in `Phantom Peak Stuff`, and 3–4 visual clues from that season's trails. The approved season name, year and Canada Water location are added by the site as a text overlay.

Each cover uses **one** part of the original Canada Water venue. Indoor scenes are based on the user's photograph of the dry, two-storey Old Town square under a black warehouse roof (`assets/phantom-peak-interior.jpg`). Outdoor scenes are based on the user's photograph of the lake, low boardwalk, cabins and tree beneath open sky (`assets/canada-water-evening.jpg`). [Phantom Peak's set designer](https://www.phantompeak.com/behind-the-scenes/tandem-set-and-scenery/) describes the original venue's warehouse and outdoor spaces. The scene references never combine the indoor galleries and theatrical roof with the outdoor lake and sky.
