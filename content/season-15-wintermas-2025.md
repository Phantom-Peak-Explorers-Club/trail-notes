# Season 15 · Wintermas 2025

These trail recaps are based on notes supplied for Wintermas 2025.

## 1. The Bright Place
Main character: Edgar Allan Crow
Other characters: Sherlock Bones, Gifford, Squawk
Themes: Film, Storytelling, Portals

Edgar Allan Crow recruits agents to protect his films from mysterious rewrites. JONACO’s frightened censor grants you access to an uncut version, where a narrator changes the story as it unfolds and leaves pages to follow. The voice belongs to Crow’s brother Squawk, trapped in The Bright Place. A clue leads to Containment Cell 3, where the portal can be opened. Sherlock Bones knows how to use KLACKY to rewrite events and rescue Squawk. You send him to the Undertakers and see the brothers reunited.

## 2. Meet The Perigates
Main character: Perigate
Other characters: Spectre, Beardy Hamm, Fishman, Platman, Gifford
Themes: Family, Disguises, Rescue

Perigate needs an actor to play their father at dinner with the Fishman. Spectre declines, but suggests Beardy Hamm. Surveillance in the Jonagraph reveals that Perigate’s real parents plan something sinister for the Fishman. You locate his containment cell and discover Platman is unavailable to save him. Platbot provides another route: turn him into an actual fish and carry him out through Gifford’s office. Perigate welcomes him home and plans a fishbowl.

## 3. A Very Merry Wintermas
Main character: Copper
Other characters: Terrence, Spectre, Sir Merrylegs, Pius
Themes: Marriage, Magic, Wintermas

Copper and Terrence have married, but Terrence vanishes while Sir Merrylegs pursues them. A severed foot and a Videomatic challenge lead you to a Gamehouse contest that you cannot win alone. Pius, the purple mage, helps you qualify as a magician. Stage magic lets you beat Merrylegs’s game and save Terrence. The attack was a plea for company: Merrylegs asks to spend Wintermas with the newlyweds and leaves Copper a gift. You pass on the request and save the celebration.

## 4. Book, Line and Sinker
Main character: Sparks
Other characters: Wendizla, Big Iggy, Jared, Tinkley Well, Captain Book, Perigate, Hogwood
Themes: Missing friends, Fairies, Ghosts

Sparks asks you to find the Lost Bois: Wendizla, Big Iggy and Jared from I.T. Clues at the Auto Hatter and waterfall point to Captain Book, whose crew you try to infiltrate through Perigate. But Tinkley Well has killed the captain. His restless ghost reveals that the fairy is responsible for the friends’ disappearance. Hogwood once belonged to the Lost Bois and offers advice, but you arrive too late to save them. To spare Sparks the truth, you say their friends have gone to Never Ever Land.

## 5. You Didn't Account For This
Main character: Stickler
Other characters: The Calculator, Finn Diesel, Sensuality Davis, Hogwood, Sparks
Themes: Espionage, Codes, University reunion

The JIA needs someone in the chair to help Stickler open a briefcase sent by their old rival, the Calculator. You guide agents Finn Diesel and Sensuality Davis through the Calculator’s lair, but they are captured. Hogwood equips you to impersonate the villain, and an office documentary points you to Sparks. A monologue reveals that Stickler and the Calculator attended university together. Their yearbook supplies the briefcase combination. Inside is an invitation to a reunion, and you persuade Stickler to attend.

## 6. The Priest And The Prisoner
Main character: Pius
Other characters: Brutus, Gifford, Terrence, Copper, Lovehart
Themes: Prison break, Siblings, Puppets

An elf delivery takes you to Pius, who wants their brother Brutus released from JONACO’s five-star prison. Gifford signs the form, but the release date is sixteen months away. Terrence and Copper help arrange a breakout instead. Once free, Brutus reveals a plan to humiliate Pius using an orbital puppetisation laser. Lovehart supplies the access codes. You puppetise Brutus and send him to the confessional booths for a reunion with Pius.

## 7. Coffee Is For Closers
Main character: Lovehart
Other characters: Marketing Bot, Stickler, Egbert Simpsbury, Salesbot
Themes: Sales, Robots, Competition

Lovehart is grieving Marketing Bot when JONACO announces a merger of Sales and Marketing. To win control for Marketing, you enter a sales contest and pitch Leg Day Ducks. Stickler tests your skill at spinning a story, while Egbert Simpsbury advises you on the final challenge. Salesbot wins anyway. You write new instructions for the dangerous robot, and Lovehart uploads them, reducing its intelligence below theirs. Lovehart becomes head of both departments.

## 8. A Developing Situation
Main character: Pocket
Other characters: Jonas, Datchery, Rexford, KLACKY, Furbish, Gifford
Themes: Mayoral politics, Popularity, The Great Undoing

After the Lunar Festival, Pocket fears Jonas will have them Undone unless their approval rating rises above seventy percent. You deliver a fruit basket to Datchery, seek an end to the platypus strike and design a new attraction with Rexford. The deeper problem is that Pocket is undoing themself. A Jonas story suggests their escape lies in arresting Furbish. With Gifford’s help you bring Furbish back from Mr Feast’s space dating show, guide the rocket to Pocket’s office and let the mayor make the arrest.

## 9. The Man With The Bowler Hat
Main character: Dr Winter
Other characters: Dr Horus, Hogwood, Littlefield
Themes: Teleportation, Toys, Lost scientist

Someone in a bowler hat is stealing the control devices from Dr Winter’s new Telebears. An obituary and a play by Hogwood identify Dr Horus, a JONACO scientist lost in a teleportation experiment. Horus contacts you through the Miramaze and asks for help returning to reality. You track down a Diamant Control Device and take it to Winter so they can save the missing doctor.

## 10. Off The Rails
Main character: Datchery
Other characters: Rexford, Myne Bland, Pocket, RoboDoc
Themes: Railway, Protest, Childhood memories

The Atlas Railroad Company plans a line that would demolish Datchery’s General Store. A protest fails, so you take a conductor job through Rexford and submit a suggestion to save the shop. CEO Myne Bland refuses. Even a staged train crash cannot stop the project. RoboDoc suggests an object from Bland’s childhood might change her heart. Datchery supplies one of the toy trains their family made. The gift does stop the railway plan, but causes Myne to explode.

## 11. Death Of A Mailman
Main character: Littlefield
Other characters: Rexford, Mr Blizzard, Friendly the Postie, Dr Winter, Spectre
Themes: Post, Mascots, Ghosts

As a new postal worker, you discover letters changing during delivery. One meant for Rexford leads to a video of the perfection-obsessed Mr Blizzard. Friendly the Postie, a mascot contest and Dr Winter point to a decades-old JONACO project led by Winter’s grandfather. Spectre explains that Blizzard’s soul needs a new puppet to escape its cycle. You create an empty puppet, giving the spirit a place to go and saving the post office.

## 12. The End Is Just The Beginning · Secret trail
Main character: Furbish
Other characters: Perigate, Sherlock Bones, Stickler, Pocket, Cogwright, Jonas
Themes: Conspiracy, Rank 8, The Great Undoing

Furbish fears an imminent apocalypse called the Great Undoing. The JONACO Metaphysics Department restricts its information to Rank 8, so you try and fail to earn that rank through Perigate. Sherlock Bones points you toward a hidden Rank 8: Stickler. They reveal two conditions for the Undoing to happen: Pocket must be mayor and Cogwright must be in town. A message from Jonas confirms that, after years of preparation, the plan is about to begin.
