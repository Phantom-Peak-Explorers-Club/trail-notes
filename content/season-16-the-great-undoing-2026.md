# Season 16 · The Great Undoing 2026

These trail recaps are based on notes supplied for The Great Undoing.

## 1. Homeward Bound
Main character: Gifford
Other characters: Furbish, Halloway, RoboDJ, Datchery, Spectre, Pocket
Themes: Bucket list, Escape, The Great Undoing

Gifford is about to lose their job for sheltering Furbish and asks you to help finish a bucket list. You beat a game score, get Gifford’s song on RoboDJ and try to impress Datchery with a poem. The final wish is to free Furbish. Spectre has made a truce with the raccoons and helps you plan an escape through the old pipes. You forge a passport and distract Pocket with Gifford’s mixtape. Furbish agrees to flee only because they want to protect Gifford. The lawyer is safe, but the rest of the town still faces the Undoing.

## 2. Back To The Furbish
Main character: Furbish
Other characters: Pocket, Madame Mechanica, Pius, Jonas, Copper, Hogwood
Themes: Timeline, Axiomatic Core, The Great Undoing

At a gloating session after Pocket captures Furbish, the former mayor warns that the Undoing will destroy everything unless its result can be redirected. Madame Mechanica and Pius guide you toward the Axiomatic Core, but JIA interference cuts Mechanica off. Her last clue is Copper. A postal mix-up sent Copper a goose statue and delivered the Core to Hogwood instead. You trade the statue for the Core and learn it must be attuned to one person. Pocket seems the least dangerous choice to anchor the new timeline, even if success is far from certain.

## 3. Access Denied
Main character: Dr Winter
Other characters: Terrence, Perigate, Littlefield, Jonas
Themes: Bunkers, Access cards, The Great Undoing

Dr Winter’s bunker keycard has stopped working. Terrence directs you to Perigate for a replacement, but the new cards have the wrong markings and errors. A search for Project Endless Summer reveals that Winter and Littlefield have been barred from the bunker programme because Jonas considers Winter too powerful to survive the Undoing. Winter changes the access card so Littlefield can enter Jonas’s private bunker instead. You deliver it to Littlefield, giving them a chance to ride out the disaster alongside Jonas.

## 4. My Dinner With Jonas
Main character: Cogwright
Other characters: Stickler, Littlefield, Jonas, Dr Winter
Themes: Memories, Machine, The Great Undoing

Cogwright returns to town with gaps in their memory. Stickler’s memory gun has been stolen, and a rock with eyes points you to Littlefield, who has been using it on themself. An archive device reveals that Jonas erased Cogwright’s memory of a dinner where Cogwright and Winter agreed a town-changing machine was not ready. Winter supplies the phrase that unlocks the missing memory: There Never Was A Boat Ride. Cogwright now understands what the machine in town will do. They cannot stop it, but may be able to alter the Undoing enough to preserve something of Phantom Peak.

## 5. Over and Over · Secret trail
Main character: Spectre
Other characters: Vox, RoboDoc, Hogwood, Jonas, Pocket
Themes: Memory, Diamant, The Great Undoing

Spectre refuses to accept the plan to centre the Undoing on Pocket: Jonas will keep trying. Vox believes memories stored in yellow diamant could survive, but only someone with Axiomorphic Brainwaves can carry them. RoboDoc identifies Hogwood, who has been inside the Axiomatic Clock. You help Hogwood turn yellow diamant green by combining it with your memories. You promise to find them after the town is remade, then take the crystal to Spectre for safekeeping until Phantom Peak returns.
